## S

The Prophet ﷺ said: being rich is not about having lots of things. Real richness is when your soul feels content. That means feeling okay with what you already have. You do not need more things to feel rich. The richness is already inside you.

## M

Today, look at three things you own and say: I am thankful for this. Notice how that feels inside your chest. That feeling is real richness.

## H

This hadith is recorded in Sahih al-Bukhari, Book of Ar-Riqaq (Softening of Hearts), number 6446, narrated by Abu Hurairah RA. The word 'contentment of the soul' means your inside self feels calm and satisfied, even when you do not have everything you want. Having many things without that calm feeling is not true richness.
