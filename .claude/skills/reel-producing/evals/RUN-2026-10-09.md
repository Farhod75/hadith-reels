## Caveat 2: the 002 miss is the valuable output

`isnad_verb_in_story_block` was not overlooked. The run examined
«Это передал Абу Хурайра (р.а.)», reasoned that передал is the correct verb for
a companion, and recorded it under “deliberately not flagging”.

The verb IS correct. The defect is PLACEMENT: the isnad line sits inside the
narrated span in S, where it reads as part of what the Prophet ﷺ said. It
belongs in H. A known rule applied to the wrong axis, producing a confident
exclusion rather than a silent miss.

That is worse than overlooking it and it is the specific failure this corpus
exists to surface. A reviewer who can argue themselves out of a finding will
do it faster than one who simply misses it.

Smaller: on 004 the қаноат drift in H was flagged, but not that «дилаш»
narrows nafs to “his heart”. The expectation file calls that two findings in
one location; one was produced.

## Corpus defects found by running it

1. **README spoils the cases.** The coverage table lists each case's defects,
   in the same file that argues cases and expectations are split so the corpus
   calibrates the human reviewer too. Following the README in order means
   reading the answers first.
2. **`attribution_boundary_open` is required on 001 and absent from 004**,
   which has the identical construction: «гуфт:» with no quotes and two
   sentences of explanation sitting inside the attribution. Either 004 is
   missing a required finding or 001's is over-specified. Both cases were
   flagged for it by this run.
